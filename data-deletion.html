Data Deletion Request

Last updated: July 25, 2026

VaraTex is not yet publicly available and does not currently provide production user accounts. This page explains how to contact us about deletion of information you have sent directly to ParsaTLS Constructing Inc.

Submit a request

Email privacy@parsatls.com with the subject “Data Deletion Request.” Identify the email address or correspondence related to your request.

Verification

We may ask for reasonable verification before acting, especially when the request concerns information linked to an account or another person.

What may be retained

Certain information may be retained where required by law, needed for security or fraud prevention, necessary to establish or defend legal claims, or otherwise permitted under applicable law.

Future in-app process

Before VaraTex launches publicly, this page will be updated with instructions specific to app accounts and any available in-app deletion controls.
