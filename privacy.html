Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026

This policy describes the current corporate website. VaraTex is still in development; this policy will be expanded before public release to describe the app’s actual data practices.

1. Who we are

ParsaTLS Constructing Inc. is a Canadian corporation. Questions about privacy may be sent to privacy@parsatls.com.

2. Information collected by this website

This static website does not currently provide user accounts, payment processing, analytics, advertising, or contact forms. When you email us, we receive the information you choose to include, such as your email address, name, and message.

3. How we use information

We use email correspondence to answer questions, provide support, respond to privacy or legal requests, and maintain business records where reasonably necessary.

4. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information to service providers that help operate our email or website, when required by law, or to protect our rights and users.

5. Retention

We retain correspondence only as long as reasonably needed for the purpose for which it was received, legal compliance, security, or recordkeeping.

6. Security

We use reasonable safeguards, but no electronic transmission or storage method is completely secure.

7. Your choices

You may ask about, correct, or request deletion of information you sent us by contacting privacy@parsatls.com.

8. Changes

We may update this policy as the website and VaraTex evolve. The revised date will appear at the top of this page.
